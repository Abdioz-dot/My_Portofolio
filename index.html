My story so far

I'm Achmad — an IT student at SMK Negeri 7 Semarang majoring in Information Systems and Networking. I got into technology because I genuinely wanted to understand what happens behind the screen when you click, connect, or send something.

These days I split my time between network labs (DHCP, NAT, hotspot, troubleshooting), web security exercises in safe environments like OWASP Juice Shop, and building small websites and IoT prototypes. I'm motivated by practical problems — if I can break it in a lab, fix it, and explain it, then I've actually learned it.

The creative side matters to me too. Playing guitar taught me patience, rhythm, and listening — surprisingly useful when you're tracing packets or debugging JavaScript at midnight.

MY TROUBLESHOOTING CHECKLIST
  1. 1. Physical → cables, LEDs, power, port status.
